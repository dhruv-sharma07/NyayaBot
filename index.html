Case 01: E-Commerce Refund Denial Consumer Law
I ordered a laptop worth ₹65,000 online. A soap was delivered, and the company is refusing refund saying return window is over. What are my options?
🏛️ Consumer Protection Act, 2019 & E-Commerce Rules, 2020:
This is an unfair trade practice and deficiency in service.

Step 1: File an immediate grievance on National Consumer Helpline (consumerhelpline.gov.in) or call 1915.
Step 2: Send a 15-day Legal Notice to the company's Grievance Officer.
Step 3: File an e-complaint on edaakhil.nic.in before the District Consumer Commission (DCDRC) for refund + compensation for mental harassment.
📍 DCDRC Jurisdiction & INGRAM Helpline
Case 02: Illegal Landlord Eviction Property Law
My landlord cut off water supply and changed the door lock without notice to force me out. What legal steps can I take?
⚖️ Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Sec 106) & Rent Control Laws:
A landlord CANNOT unilaterally disconnect essential services or forcibly evict a tenant without due process.

Immediate Action: Dial 112. Cutting utilities constitutes criminal intimidation (IPC Sec 503/506 / BNS Sec 351) and criminal trespass.
Civil Remedy: File for an immediate restoration injunction before the Rent Authority / Civil Court.
📍 IPC 503 / BNS 351 + Rent Authority Injunction
Case 03: Cyber Blackmail & Extortion Cyber Law
Someone from a fake Instagram account is threatening to leak morphed photos to my contacts unless I pay ₹20,000. Please help!
🚨 IT Act 2000 (Sec 66E, 67, 67A) & IPC Sec 384 (Extortion) / BNS 308:
DO NOT PAY: Paying money never stops blackmailers.

1. Preserve screenshots with visible handles and timestamps.
2. Report immediately to cybercrime.gov.in under "Women/Children Online Crime" or call helpline 1930.
3. Use the National Cyber Cell portal to block hashes of private images.
📍 IT Act 66E/67A + Helpline 1930
Case 04: Police Refusing to File FIR Criminal Procedure
I went to the police station to file an FIR for robbery, but the SHO refused and only gave a simple diary entry (DD entry). What can I do?
👮 CrPC Section 154 / BNSS Section 173 (Lalita Kumari Mandate):
Registration of an FIR is mandatory for cognizable offences.

Remedy 1: Send your written complaint to the Superintendent of Police (SP) via Registered Speed Post under CrPC 154(3) / BNSS 173(4).
Remedy 2: If SP fails to direct an FIR, file an application before the Judicial Magistrate under CrPC 156(3) / BNSS 175(3).
📍 CrPC 154(3), 156(3) & BNSS 173
